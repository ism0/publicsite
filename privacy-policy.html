Privacy Policy – Hoplin

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Hoplin is a game by GizmoByte Games.

Hoplin does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

  • The game does not use the internet. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no in-app purchases.
  • Your progress, settings, high scores and the names you type for the high-score lists are saved only on your own device. They are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes them.
  • Android may back up the game's save to your own Google account through Android's Auto Backup, if you have backup turned on. This is controlled by your device settings, not by the game.
  • The only permission the game asks for is vibration, used for feedback during play.
  • The "Buy me a coffee" button on the INFO page opens a web page in your browser. What happens on that site is covered by that site's own privacy policy.

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
